HAVE YOU BEEN SUED?

Civil Lawsuit Defense for Private Individuals

Being named in a civil lawsuit can place your savings, property, business interests, reputation, and future at risk. Many civil lawsuits are not covered by insurance, leaving the defendant responsible for finding and retaining counsel. Under California law, a defendant generally has 30 days from the date of service to respond to a complaint — missing that window can result in a default judgment being entered against you before you have had any real opportunity to defend yourself.

RV Litigation Group PC represents private individuals, professionals, guarantors, investors, property owners, and business principals who have been sued and need immediate, strategic representation in San Jose, San Francisco, Palo Alto, Oakland, and throughout Santa Clara County and San Francisco County.

Please note: private-client civil matters are handled on an hourly and retainer basis. We do not accept contingency matters or civil-rights cases.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept contingency-fee civil cases?

No. RV Litigation Group PC handles private civil matters on an hourly and retainer basis. We do not accept contingency-fee matters.

Will my insurance cover the lawsuit against me?

It depends on the claim. Many private civil matters — including contract disputes, personal guarantee claims, and neighbor disputes — are not covered by any insurance policy. Some claims may trigger coverage under a homeowners, umbrella, or business policy, and we can help you review your policies to find out.

Do you handle civil rights, police misconduct, or Section 1983 cases?

No. RV Litigation Group PC does not handle civil rights, police misconduct, prisoner-rights, Section 1983, or constitutional-rights claims.
