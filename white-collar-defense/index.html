When to involve counsel

An investigator requests an interview, a subpoena seeks company records, or questions arise about transactions and financial controls. Representation can begin before a charge is filed. We assess whose interests are involved and whether the company and an individual need separate counsel.

Our approach

We clarify the status of the investigation, identify immediate obligations, and develop a response based on the records and allegations. Preparation may include reviewing transactions, preserving relevant information, communicating with investigators through counsel, and evaluating a negotiated resolution or defense in court.

Before we begin

Questions & Answers

Can I contact you before charges are filed?

Yes. We review matters at the investigation stage as well as after charges are filed. Include any interview, subpoena, or court deadline in your initial inquiry.

Can one lawyer represent the business and an executive?

That depends on the circumstances and potential conflicts. We review representation separately before agreeing to act for a company or an individual.

Do you still handle other criminal matters?

Selected criminal defense matters remain available through our secondary practice page. Contact us to confirm whether we can consider your matter.
