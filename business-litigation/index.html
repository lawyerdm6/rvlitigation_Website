When to involve counsel

A key agreement has broken down. An owner is blocking decisions or diverting business. A demand letter or lawsuit threatens operations. We help assess the legal position and the practical consequences together, including cost, timing, evidence, and the ongoing commercial relationship.

Our approach

We begin with the contracts, communications, deadlines, and business objectives. From there, we develop a plan for preserving evidence, evaluating claims and defenses, and pursuing an appropriate resolution. Negotiation and litigation preparation can proceed together; the right path depends on the facts and the client’s goals.

Questions & Answers

Do you represent businesses bringing and defending claims?

Yes. We evaluate both claims a business may pursue and allegations made against a business or its owners.

Do you represent employees in workplace claims?

No. Our employment practice represents businesses and employers. We do not take employee-side employment matters.

How are litigation fees handled?

Our business litigation work is handled on a retainer basis, not a contingency basis. Scope and fee terms are discussed before an engagement is accepted.
