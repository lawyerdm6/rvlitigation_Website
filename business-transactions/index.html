When to involve counsel

You are signing a significant customer or vendor agreement, bringing a new owner into the company, or negotiating a purchase or sale. We help identify the decisions that should be made before signing, including responsibilities, payment, control, and what happens if the relationship changes.

Our approach

We learn how the business operates and what the transaction needs to accomplish. We review proposed terms, flag unresolved obligations, and work through negotiation and documentation. When tax or dispute questions overlap, we coordinate the legal work so those issues are considered together.

Before we begin

Questions & Answers

Can you review an agreement before I sign it?

Yes. Share a brief description of the agreement and any signing deadline when you contact us. We will confirm the scope and availability before accepting the work.

Can you work with my accountant or other advisers?

Yes. With your authorization, we can coordinate legal questions with your other advisers and clarify who is responsible for each part of the transaction.

Can you help after a business relationship breaks down?

Our Business Litigation practice evaluates disputes. We can help determine whether the immediate need is a revised agreement, negotiation, or litigation.
