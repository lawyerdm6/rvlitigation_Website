Questions & Answers

What are your primary practice areas?

Business Litigation, Business Transactions, Tax Law, and White-Collar Defense.

Do you accept employee-side employment matters?

No. Employment representation is limited to businesses and employers.

Do you handle small claims or other criminal matters?

Yes, we consider small-claims advice and appeals and selected criminal defense. Acceptance depends on the matter, conflicts, and availability.

Do you represent individuals in tax matters?

Yes. Tax Law serves both individuals and businesses.

What happens after an inquiry?

The firm reviews the matter for fit and conflicts and discusses the next step. An inquiry alone does not create representation or confirm that a deadline will be handled.

Are consultations free?

Contact the office to discuss the consultation and any applicable fee. No fee or engagement terms are promised by submitting the form.

Where is the office?

28 Geary St, Suite 650, San Francisco, CA 94108. We serve clients throughout California; please arrange meetings with the office.
