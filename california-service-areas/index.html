Start with the matter, then the location.

A Fairfield business owner may need help reviewing a demand letter before litigation begins. An individual in Sacramento may need to respond to a lawsuit or discuss a pending criminal charge. These are hypothetical situations, not firm cases or results. The documents, parties, type of proceeding and upcoming dates determine the next steps.

When you contact us, include the type of matter, city or county, whether a case has been filed and any known deadline. We will discuss whether the matter fits our practice and what information is needed for a conflicts review. Please keep sensitive records out of the initial inquiry.
