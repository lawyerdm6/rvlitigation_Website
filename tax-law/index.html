When to involve counsel

A tax notice arrives, an audit expands, or an unresolved disagreement moves toward an appeal. Owners may also need to understand how a tax issue affects the business and their own position. We help identify the stage of the matter, the information needed, and the legal response to consider.

Our approach

We start with the notice, the agency involved, the tax periods, and the response dates. We assess the available record and work with you and, when authorized, your accounting advisers. If the matter involves potential criminal exposure, we coordinate tax and defense strategy before a response is made.
