A focused scope for smaller disputes

We consider small-claims advice and appeal representation. During intake, we discuss the amount at issue, the stage of the case, and the likely scope and cost of legal work. We do not assume that hiring counsel is the most practical approach for every small-claims dispute.

Understand the stage of your case

Attorneys generally do not represent parties at the initial California small-claims hearing. On appeal, either side can have a lawyer at the new trial.

An appeal is generally available to a party ordered to pay on the other party’s claim, not to challenge the loss of that party’s own claim. If you missed the hearing, a request to vacate may be the relevant step instead.

California Courts states that a notice of appeal must generally be filed within 30 days after the Notice of Entry of Judgment was handed or mailed to you. Ask counsel to review the documents promptly; do not calculate your deadline from this page alone.

Questions & Answers

What should I include in an inquiry?

Tell us whether a hearing or judgment has occurred, whether you are bringing or defending an appeal, the court, and any known deadline.

Do you promise representation at the initial hearing?

No. The initial small-claims hearing generally requires parties to appear without attorney representation. We discuss the permissible scope of assistance during intake.

Will you review whether an appeal makes sense?

Yes. We consider the stage, the issues, and the practical cost of representation before accepting a matter.
